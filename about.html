ABOUT THE PROJECT

Making salary information easier to explore

SalaryPredict is a simple website concept that helps people understand the factors that can influence salary expectations.

◎

Our purpose

The project is designed to provide an indicative salary estimate based on professional details such as job role, experience, education, industry, region, and company size.

⌘

Who can use it?

Employees can use estimates as a starting point to research fair pay, while employers and students can explore broad compensation patterns.

↗

How it works

The current recreated demo uses a rule-based illustrative calculation in JavaScript. It does not connect to a live salary database or trained machine-learning model.

Important note

Results are for educational demonstration only. Actual salaries depend on skills, employer, location, market conditions, and many other factors.
